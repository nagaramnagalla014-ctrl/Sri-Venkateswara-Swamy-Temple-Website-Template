స్వాగతంWelcome to the Temple

Vanchakalpalathasiddhi Sri Padmavathi, Goda Devi sameta Sri Venkateswara Swamy vari Devasthanam is located in Narayanapuram Colony, Poranki. Sri Swamy vari Prathishta was performed on 27 January 2007, the holy day of Bhishma Ekadasi.

Daily darshan, weekly sevas, the monthly Sravana Nakshatram Kalyanam and the annual Varshika Utsavalu are performed with devotion. All devotees are welcome.
